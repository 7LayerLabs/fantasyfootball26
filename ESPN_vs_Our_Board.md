Compared 2026-08-12 against ESPN's staff consensus position rankings (8 analysts, updated Aug 11) and Field Yates' overall PPR Top 160 (updated Aug 5). Our board: 2026_Fantasy_Football_Draft_Engine_100_PERCENT_FIXED.xlsx, 223 players.

## Headline numbers

- Overall agreement with ESPN's Top 160: correlation 0.895 (1.000 would be identical, 0 would be random).
- Inside ESPN's top 60 (the first six rounds), our typical player sits within 12 overall spots of ESPN, about one round of a 10-team draft.
- Position order agreement (average spots apart per player): QB 3.0, RB 3.0, WR 4.1, TE 1.6, K 2.5, DST 2.5.
- Coverage: we matched 151 of ESPN's 160 overall names and every player on their QB and DST lists.

## How to read this

Nobody knows who is right until the games are played. What this measures is how far our board stands from the consensus most draft rooms follow. The agreement is strong, which means the ratings are sane and market-anchored. The gaps are specific opinions, and on draft day they work in our favor two ways: players we like more than ESPN will still be on the board when we want them (the room drafts off ESPN, so we get our guys at a discount), and players ESPN likes more than us simply get taken by someone else before we would have reached. The only real exposure is the missing-player list above and any of our bets built on stale information.
